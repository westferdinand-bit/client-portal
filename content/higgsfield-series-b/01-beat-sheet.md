# Higgsfield Series B — 45s Vertical Cartoon Explainer
**Format:** 9:16, 45 seconds · **Style:** Pixar-style 3D, side-scrolling platformer set in cartoon New York City
**Narrator:** Pip (original cartoon creature) · **Angle:** AI-space awareness · **CTA:** Follow

---

## The world
Cartoon New York City, staged as a side-scrolling platformer level. Golden-hour light,
brownstone stoops, fire escapes as ledges, subway grates venting steam, yellow cabs,
bodega awnings, pigeons.

NYC is not decoration here — it does the argument's work. The city gives us two real
districts that mean something without a word of explanation: **Times Square** (spectacle,
neon, tourists, novelty) and **the Financial District** (stone, steel, institutions,
permanence). Pip running from one to the other *is* the toy-to-tool crossing. And a city
that only goes up is the right place to stage a number that went up 35x.

All signage stays blank — no lettering, no real brands.

## The one idea
AI video generation just crossed from novelty to infrastructure — and the revenue mix is
the proof. That's the single thing the viewer should walk away knowing.

No business-vertical framing. No "here's how to use it for your company." This is a
heads-up about where the AI space actually is right now.

## Hook (contrarian, "you" forward)
> **"If you think the AI boom is cooling off, look at this number."**

Alternates:
- *"A company you've probably never heard of just did something no company has done this fast."*
- *"Two numbers tell you exactly where AI is right now."*

## Beat sheet

### BEAT 1 — HOOK · 0:00–0:04
**Visual:** Cold open, Pip already mid-run, side-on, sprinting right along a golden-hour
brownstone block — stoops, railings, a steaming subway grate, a yellow cab blurring past.
Retro HUD in the corner. Level banner drops: `THE AI BOOM`.
**On-screen text:** `Still think AI is cooling off?`
**VO:** "If you think the AI boom is cooling off, look at this number."
**Note:** First frame must read in motion — Pip already running, never standing still.

### BEAT 2 — THE JUMP · 0:04–0:18
**Visual:** Pip lands on a low brownstone stoop stamped `$20M`. Beat of stillness. Then
the camera cranes up hard and keeps going, past fire escapes and glowing windows, revealing
a colossal skyscraper far above stamped `$700M` — its facade stacked with game platforms
like a level nobody has climbed. Pip is a speck. His antenna orb flares.
**On-screen text:** `$20M → $700M · 12 months`
**VO:** "Higgsfield just raised four hundred million dollars. A year ago it made twenty
million. Today it's at seven hundred million a year. That's thirty-five times, in twelve
months."

### BEAT 3 — THE SCALE · 0:18–0:24
**Visual:** Cut to a subway station. Turnstiles spin and spin; a departure-board counter
rolls up to `30,000,000`. Coins cascade out of the turnstiles. Pip's arms full.
**On-screen text:** `30M users · 200 countries`
**VO:** "Thirty million people use it. And here's the part that actually matters —"

### BEAT 4 — THE CROSSING · 0:24–0:38
**Visual:** Pip runs right and crosses a hard line in the sidewalk. Behind him: Times
Square — neon, spectacle, bright plastic arcade platforms, confetti. Ahead: the Financial
District — stone columns, steel, brass, long shadows between towers. Grey suited NPC
silhouettes are already at work there. Windows light up one by one across the skyline until
a counter ticks to `390`.
**On-screen text:** `390 of the Fortune 500`
**VO:** "Three hundred ninety of the Fortune Five Hundred are on it, and businesses now
make up most of the revenue. That's the line between a toy and a tool."

### BEAT 5 — CTA · 0:38–0:45
**Visual:** Pip plants a flag on a Financial District rooftop, whole skyline behind him at
dusk, windows glowing. `LEVEL COMPLETE` card. He turns and points at camera, antenna glowing.
**On-screen text:** `Follow for more`
**VO:** "AI video just crossed it. Follow — I'll keep you ahead of this stuff."

## Runtime check
~95 words. Spoken numbers eat more time than their word count suggests, so budget ≈2.2 wps
= **43 seconds.** If it runs long, cut "That's thirty-five times, in twelve months" — the
visual already says it.

## Audio
- Music: upbeat 8-bit / orchestral hybrid, no lyrics.
- SFX: jump (B2), rising whoosh under the crane-up (B2 — it lands flat without one),
  turnstile clatter + coin cascade (B3), a low bass thud as Pip crosses the line and the
  Times Square noise cuts to near-silence (B4), level-complete fanfare (B5).
- City bed under everything: distant traffic, a far-off siren, pigeon wingbeats. Duck it
  hard at the Beat 4 crossing — the silence is what sells the change.
- Mix VO 6dB above bed.

## Art direction notes
- Golden hour throughout Beats 1-3; dusk by Beat 5. The light moving is the runtime moving.
- Blank signage everywhere. No lettering except the deliberate game text (`$20M`, `$700M`,
  `390`, `LEVEL COMPLETE`), which gets added in post, not generated.
- Pip's design is locked — see `02-pip-character-sheet.md`. The city changes around him;
  he does not change.
