## How to audition properly
Don't judge on the stock preview. Judge on **line 10**: *"That's the line between a toy and a
tool."* It's the thesis, it has to land flat and certain, and it's the line most likely to come
out either smug or lifeless. A voice that nails that one will carry the other eleven.

Say the word and I'll render the full 95-word read in your top two so you can compare on the
real script instead of the demo.
